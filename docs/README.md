# 🎒 Tomorrow's Bag

A simple app to help your child pack the right notebooks for school —
without the "wait, do you have your Science book?" scramble every morning.

**Try it here:** https://akrishna87.github.io/Akrishna87/docs/

## First-time setup (2 minutes)

1. Open the link above and tap **🧑 Parent** at the top.
2. Under **Names & class**, fill in your child's name, their class/grade,
   your name, and the school's location. This makes the app greet your
   child by name.
3. Scroll to **School holidays** — it already has DPS Gurgaon's holiday
   dates for this year loaded in. Take a quick look and remove/add any
   that don't match your child's school.
4. (Optional) Under **Usual weekly plan**, tap each day of the week and
   pick the subjects that are usually needed that day — the app will then
   suggest them automatically each evening, so your child mostly just has
   to confirm rather than pick from scratch.

## Every evening

Your child opens the app (it's already on **🧒 Kid** mode) and simply taps
the notebooks/subjects they need for tomorrow, adds a note if there's
anything extra (like PE shoes), and taps **"All Packed!"**.

## Every morning (for you)

Tap **🧑 Parent** any time to see what your child said they packed for
today, at a glance — no need to ask them again.

## Things to try while testing

- Does the greeting and class show up correctly for your child?
- Do the weekday suggestions match what's actually needed?
- If tomorrow is a school holiday, does the app correctly say so?
- Is anything confusing, missing, or annoying to tap through?

## A few honest notes

- This is saved only on the device/browser you're using it on — if you
  open it on a different phone or browser, it starts fresh. (We're
  testing it this way for now; nothing is lost, it just doesn't sync
  between devices yet.)
- The holiday dates are entered by hand from the school's own calendar —
  please double check them against what your school has actually shared.
- This is still an early test version — if something feels off or you
  think of something that would make it more useful, please share it!
